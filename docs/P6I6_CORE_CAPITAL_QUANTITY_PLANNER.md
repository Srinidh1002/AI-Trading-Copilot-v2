# P6I-6 Core capital and quantity planner

## Purpose
Build deterministic BLOCKED, NO_SIZE or READY planning results from one effective-constraints resolution.
## Selection rule
The planner chooses the minimum raw affordability/risk limits and policy maximum, then compares it to the policy minimum.
## Boundary
P6I-7 allocates only already-planned lots. No charges, taxes, brokerage, slippage, orders or execution.
