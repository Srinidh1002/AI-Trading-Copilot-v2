# X9-B2 — Five-market research comparability, not market selection

This phase adds `services/x9/research_eligibility_v1.py`, consuming exact unchanged X9 ledger, X9 session matrix and **all five** X8 readiness/description pairs. For every market, it checks the original readiness hash against the corresponding X9 slot, recomputes its X8 description, and binds the session evidence to the identical readiness hash and parent-cycle timestamp. It requires each market exactly once and emits the fixed order NIFTY, SENSEX, CRUDEOILM, GOLDM, NATGASMINI irrespective of input tuple order.

Per-market statuses are `COMPARABLE_CONTEXT`, `EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`, `SESSION_UNKNOWN` and `REPORTED_CLOSED`. These are **research states**, not trading eligibility or canonical exchange-open truth. `COMPARABLE_CONTEXT` requires a ready-for-classification X8 description and a caller-reported open session. The original exchange/calendar authority remains external, so caller-supplied session reports cannot authorize an order. A market reported closed remains visible instead of being dropped. Five complete descriptive slots do not imply five simultaneously tradable markets.

`X9ResearchEligibilityV1` carries all original X8 and session hashes, `comparable_market_count` and explicit completeness. `selected_market` is always None; `rankings` is always empty. No arbitrary scoring weights, opportunity confidence, CALL/PUT signal, cross-market tie breaking or exposure allocation exists in B2. Future comparative scoring requires a separately tested independent-evidence and policy audit. This subsystem has zero risk, position, execution or certification authority.
