# P6I-9A Trading cost input attachment

## Scope
Attaches typed P6I-8 policy and caller evidence to the immutable P6I input.
## Coherence
Evidence identity, policy ID, planning ID, trade-plan ID, selection-result ID and PAPER fields must agree.
## Boundary
No trading cost is calculated, deducted, or applied to capital, lots, quantity, outlay, risk, status, orders or execution.
