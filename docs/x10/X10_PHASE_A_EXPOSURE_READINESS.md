`build_x10_portfolio_readiness_v1` requires all five market slots and the immutable X9 parent ledger, rejects future records, orders results canonically and binds the full X9 digest. Total reported counts are computed only if each market has a reported count and the same source-record pair is not reused across markets. Shared-source groups are disclosed and totals become `None` rather than double-counted. A complete research inventory never establishes financial solvency, loss limits, buying power, hedge offsets or order admission. `deployable_capital=None`, `risk_budget=None`, `capital_admission_allowed=False`; all execution, risk, position, certification and live-authority fields are fixed False.
